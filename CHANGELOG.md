# Changelog

## 1.0.0

The first release.

- A small Christmas tree that floats above every window, on every desktop and over full-screen apps. Drag it anywhere and it stays there.
- 14 tree designs, from Classic Red & Gold to Gold Wire, drawn branch by branch with twinkling lights and a glowing star.
- Snowfall around the tree, on or off.
- Christmas carols played on a synthesized music box: Jingle Bells, We Wish You a Merry Christmas, Deck the Halls, Silent Night, and Joy to the World.
- English and Korean, following the macOS language.
- Automatic updates.
